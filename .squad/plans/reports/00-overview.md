## Dependency notes

- **Story 08 depends on Story 01** for `lib/prisma.ts`, `auth.ts`, `app/providers.tsx`, `app/agent/layout.tsx`, `app/portal/layout.tsx`, and `components/agent/sidebar-nav.tsx`. **On Story 03** (commit `ea52bab`) for **`requireAdmin()`** (`lib/api/http.ts:19–24`) and `lib/api/client.ts`'s `request<T>()`/`ApiError`. **On Story 04** (commit `cd32c28`) for **`Customer.userId`**, which is the sole ownership check the feedback route may use. **On Story 05** (commit `ee1482f`) for the `Ticket` model, `lib/sla.ts`, `lib/ticket-access.ts`'s `resolveViewer()`, and the ticket routes it extends. **On Story 06** (commit `e148a5f`) for `requireUser()`. **On Story 07** (commit `4a732d1`) for `lib/ticket-select.ts`, and for `app/api/dashboard/route.ts` + `lib/dashboard.ts` as the structural template the reports endpoint and client module copy.
- **This is the story that takes Story 07's deferrals.** Story 07's overview explicitly reserved *charts and aggregate reporting* and *de-duplicating `TICKET_DETAIL_SELECT`* for a later story; Story 08 is that story and does both.
- **Story 08 is not read-only, despite the intake's wording.** The intake says everything but the feedback submission reads existing data. Two of its acceptance criteria — "% of tickets resolved within their `dueAt` target" and "average resolution time" — are **not computable from the current schema**: `Ticket` has no resolution timestamp, and `updatedAt` (`prisma/schema.prisma:86`) is not one, because it moves on every later edit. The story therefore adds **one nullable column, `Ticket.resolvedAt`**, plus the transition-only write that maintains it. This is a deliberate, recorded deviation — a diff without it cannot satisfy the SLA criteria, and a later story must not "revert it to spec".
- **Shared contracts introduced by Story 08** — later stories consume these rather than redefining them:
  - **`Feedback`** (`prisma/schema.prisma`) — one row per ticket, enforced by `@unique` on `ticketId`, `onDelete: Cascade`. **No author column**: the author is `ticket.customer` by construction, because the route refuses every other caller. There is exactly one writer, `POST /api/tickets/[id]/feedback`, and no update or delete endpoint.
  - **`Ticket.resolvedAt`** — nullable, written **only on the transition into `TERMINAL_STATUSES`** and cleared by the reopen action. `RESOLVED → CLOSED` does **not** rewrite it; a PATCH that does not mention `status` does not touch it. A re-resolved ticket gets a **new** stamp, so the reports measure the most recent resolution. Legacy rows are backfilled from `updatedAt` and are explicitly an approximation.
  - `lib/ticket-select.ts` gains **`TICKET_DETAIL_SELECT`**, lifted out of the two route files that held byte-identical copies, and it now selects `feedback`. Story 07 left this duplicated on purpose; Story 08 closes it because a `feedback` key added to one copy and not the other is a silent runtime bug.
  - `lib/report-metrics.ts` → **`summariseSla()`**, **`summariseAgents()`** and **`formatDuration()`**. **Pure** — no Prisma, no `Request` — like `describeTicketChanges` in `lib/activity.ts:77–127`, and imported by client components. A `@/lib/prisma` import in it fails `npm run build`, not `npm run dev`.
  - `app/api/reports/route.ts` → **`GET /api/reports`**, a composite `{ tickets, sla, csat, terminalStatuses }` payload following `GET /api/dashboard`'s shape.
  - `app/api/reports/agents/route.ts` → **`GET /api/reports/agents`**, a **separate, `requireAdmin()`-gated** route. Kept off `/api/reports` on purpose, so the admin-only data has an admin-only endpoint rather than a conditionally-populated field.
  - `lib/reports.ts` and `lib/feedback.ts` → the client modules (`ReportSummary`, `AgentPerformance`, `reportKeys`, `TicketFeedback`, `postFeedback`), following the `lib/dashboard.ts` shape.
  - `lib/validation/feedback.ts` → **`RATING_MIN` / `RATING_MAX` / `RATING_VALUES`** and `createFeedbackSchema`. The rating bounds live here and nowhere else, exactly as `TICKET_STATUSES` does in `lib/validation/ticket.ts`.
  - **`recharts@^3.10.1`** — the repo's first charting library, and the only new dependency. Chart components are client-only and take their colours from the existing `--chart-1` … `--chart-5` variables (`app/globals.css:70–74`, redefined for dark mode at 105–109).
- **Behaviour introduced by Story 08** — later stories must assume these semantics:
  - **Feedback is customer-owner-only.** Staff get `403`; a customer who does not own the ticket gets **`404`**, not `403`, matching `app/api/tickets/[id]/comments/route.ts:23` — a 403 would confirm the id exists. Ownership resolves through `Customer.userId` and **never** through email.
  - **Feedback is terminal-only and once-only.** `409` before resolution, `409` on a second submission, including the concurrent case via `P2002`. The handler check is the friendly path; the `@unique` constraint is the guarantee.
  - **An empty comment stores `null`, never `""`.**
  - **Feedback writes no `AuditLog` row and no `Notification`.** `AuditLog` is the trail of ticket *mutations* and feedback mutates no ticket field; notifying an assignee of a rating is a product decision the intake does not make. Both omissions are deliberate.
  - **`GET /api/tickets/[id]` now carries `feedback`.** Purely additive — no field was removed and no status code changed.
  - **`GET /api/reports/agents` returns `403` to a plain AGENT.** The admin section is gated at the data endpoint, not by hiding a component; `middleware.ts` excludes `/api/**` (matcher, line 37), so that guard is the only enforcement. The `isAdmin` prop on the page is cosmetic.
  - **Missing data is `null`, not `0`.** `sla.onTimeRate`, `sla.averageResolutionMs` and `csat.average` are all `null` when there is nothing to measure, and the UI renders `—`. "No measurable tickets" and "nothing was on time" are different facts.
  - **A ticket resolved with `dueAt: null` counts toward `resolved` but not toward `measured`** — no target means it cannot be late, which is why `onTimeRate` has its own denominator. A ticket resolved while unassigned lands in an **"Unassigned"** bucket rather than being dropped, so the per-agent counts still sum to the total.
  - **Reports are point-in-time and untransacted.** Same reasoning as `app/api/dashboard/route.ts:27–29`: a read-only report does not justify holding SQLite's write lock. Do not "fix" this with `$transaction`.
  - **Resolution durations are averaged in TypeScript over an unbounded `findMany`.** Prisma cannot average a difference of two columns and SQLite offers no aggregate for it. `@@index([resolvedAt])` keeps the scan narrow. The upgrade path when scale demands it is one `$queryRaw` with `AVG(julianday(...))` — **not** pagination, which would silently bias the average.
- **Deferred out of Story 08:**
  - **CSV/PDF export and scheduled or emailed report delivery** — named in the intake's out-of-scope list.
  - **Historical trend charts.** Every number here is point-in-time; there is no snapshot table and no date-range filter.
  - **Feedback moderation, agent replies to feedback, and any survey beyond the single post-resolution rating.**
  - **Showing the submitted rating on the agent-side ticket detail.** The data is already on the shared payload; only the portal renders it today.
  - **A customer-visible CSAT figure.** `GET /api/reports` is `403` for a CUSTOMER.
- **Still no automated test framework.** Stories 01–07 all deferred it and Story 08's intake does not ask for one, so this ships a manual + `curl` test plan. `summariseSla`, `summariseAgents` and `formatDuration` are pure and dependency-free for the same reason `authorizeAssignmentChange` and `describeTicketChanges` are — they are the arithmetic that most wants the first test suite this repo grows.
